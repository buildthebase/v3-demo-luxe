# Baby shower photo lettering restoration

Prepared for the user-approved live release on October 29, 2026. Built-in image generation was used, not the API or CLI fallback.

The full generated image was rejected because its tiny menu lettering differed from the original. Only its restored Café Bébé script was retained, feathered and colour-matched into the original full-resolution photograph. The original menu pixels were retained with restrained local sharpening. No menu items were reconstructed or guessed. The smallest print remains limited by the original footage.

Final master: `outputs/case-studies/baby-shower-lettering-master-v1.png`.

Public assets: `public/images/case-studies/baby-shower-cart-portrait-lettering-v4-*` and `public/images/case-studies/baby-shower-cart-landscape-lettering-v3-*`, plus their WebP fallbacks. Existing portrait and landscape framing is unchanged. Responsive AVIF sources serve an appropriately sized image rather than the master.

## Built-in edit prompt

Use case: precise-object-edit. Edit target: the supplied original portrait photograph of an actual coffee cart at a baby shower. Make a restrained professional photographic restoration focused ONLY on the lettering on the front of the cart and on the menu stand. Preserve the full portrait framing, camera angle, all object shapes and positions, cart stripes, bow illustration, colours, lighting, background greenery, lamps, floor and every other detail. The cart front text must remain exactly 'Café Bébé', in the same elegant lavender-blue handwritten script, same size, perspective and placement, but with crisp clean strokes and improved local contrast. On the existing menu stand make the large title 'COFFEE MENU' and the existing section headings 'SELECTED DRINKS', 'MILK OPTIONS', 'LATTE FLAVOURS', 'SAUCE FLAVOURS' cleaner, sharper and readable, preserving their small tasteful serif lettering and existing layout, cup illustration, colours and perspective. Do NOT invent, add, remove or replace any menu items, prices or small-print wording. If tiny lettering cannot be recovered confidently, preserve it as it is rather than hallucinating words. Do not restyle the photograph, enlarge the signs, move anything, remove objects or add artificial sharpening halos. Photorealistic high fidelity restoration, natural detail. Output a high resolution portrait image matching the original 9:16 composition.
